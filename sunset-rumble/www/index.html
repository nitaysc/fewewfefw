Put the phone flat between you. You drive the bottom half, your friend drives the top half.
On a keyboard: WASD + F drift + G boost vs arrows + . drift + / boost.

How to play

  • ⭐ Grab stars all over town. First to 20 wins, or most stars when the clock runs out.
  • 💥 Ram your rival at speed to knock up to 3 of their stars loose, then grab them.
  • 🌀 Hold drift while turning to slide round corners and charge your boost. A long drift gives a free burst.
  • 🚀 Hold boost to burn the meter. Blue arrows on the road and big jumps also fill it.
  • 💖 Pink stars are worth 3.

Keyboard: WASD/arrows to drive, Space drift, Shift boost, Esc pause.
