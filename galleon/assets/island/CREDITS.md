# Island assets

All from [Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com), licensed **CC0** (public domain), so they can be
published in this repository. Fetched by `tools/fetch_island_assets.py` and reduced to phone
budgets by `tools/build_island_assets.py` (decimated meshes, thinned leaf cards, 1K textures).

- Trees: Island Tree 01, 02, 03
- Rocks: Coastal Cliff 01, 02; Coast Rocks 01, 05
- Ground textures (`ground/`): Coast Sand 01, Damp Beach Sand, Forest Leaves 02, Aerial Rocks 02, Coral Ground 02
