# Paid Godot 4.x FPS templates/kits comparable to Cowsins' FPS Engine (+ Multiplayer Add-On)

Research date: 2026-11-03. All facts below come from listing pages fetched or search snippets returned on that date. itch.io shows prices, update times and dates as relative values ("22 days ago"), and sale prices change often. Treat the prices as a snapshot.

## What paid Godot 4 FPS templates exist, and what are their facts (price, license, version, features, multiplayer, mobile, reputation)?

### Takeaway
No paid Godot 4 kit matches FPS Engine's breadth. None I found combines modern movement (slide/wallrun/dash), ADS, recoil, AI and multiplayer. The paid market is small and mostly on itch.io. It consists of single-player boomer-shooter kits (Ultimate Retro Shooter Template at $18; Retro FPS Starter Kit at $9.99, on sale for $7.49) and modest modern-FPS templates (RNB PRO FPS & Enemy AI, $25 list price, on sale for $16.25; Dev.Aki FPS Zombie, $5; NeonDev Stylized FPS Kit, €12). The closest to FPS Engine's modern-weapon focus is RNB Games' PRO FPS template, which has recoil, reload, scope zoom, weapon drop/switch, FSM AI and menus. It has no ADS, no advanced movement, no multiplayer and no mobile support. None of the paid FPS kits I found ship multiplayer or touch controls.

### Inferences
- **Fit against FPS Engine:** RNB PRO FPS (about $16 to $25) is the closest paid single-player base for modern weapons. Ultimate Retro Shooter ($18) and Retro FPS Starter Kit ($7.49 to $9.99) are the most polished and best rated, but they are sprite-based boomer shooters, not an FPS Engine-style modern shooter.
- **Multiplayer, mobile and advanced movement are all missing:** no paid kit covers any of them. A user wanting FPS Engine plus its Multiplayer Add-On in Godot would probably need to combine pieces. Candidate pieces are a kit, Godot's built-in high-level multiplayer (ENet/WebSocket/WebRTC), and possibly a dedicated-server template or bukkbeek's ENet LAN template, plus a touch-controls kit. Slide, wallrun and dash would need to be added by hand.
- **Public GitHub repos:**
  - MIT kits allow this: Dev.Aki, and RNB's code (asset licenses unconfirmed).
  - GPLv3 kits (NeonDev) allow it only if the whole project is distributed under the GPL.
  - The FelixarStudio and heyheythere licenses forbid redistributing the template or its assets, so putting their source in a public repo would likely breach the terms. A private repo, or a public repo with those files excluded, would be needed. Confirm with each author.
- **Maturity:** the Godot paid-template market is far smaller than Unity's. Ratings counts are tiny (0 to 6), so quality is hard to judge from sales signals.

### Gaps
- Gumroad, Ko-fi, Patreon, Fab, GameDev Market and Humble were not checked directly. Search returned no Godot 4 FPS kits from those storefronts, but this is not proof that none exist.
- Exact release and last-update dates are missing for most itch listings, because the pages use relative times.
- No sales numbers are public on itch.io. No YouTube reviews or Reddit r/godot reviews of these paid kits were found within the research budget.
- Edic's, bitkiln's and the NYXZ Studios kits were not inspected in detail. NYXZ's page now returns 404.
- The third-party asset licenses inside RNB's kit, and the Jumpingeyes template's license, are unknown.

## Is there any Godot kit made by Cowsins, or a Godot port of FPS Engine?

### Takeaway
I found no Godot product by Cowsins and no Godot port of FPS Engine. A similarly named, unrelated "Godot FPS Engine" by Finn exists on itch.io, but its page is now password-protected.

### Cited Findings
- A search for "Cowsins Godot FPS engine" returned no Cowsins-attributed Godot asset. — [search results incl. finnder listing](https://finnder.itch.io/godot-fps-engine)
- "Godot FPS Engine" by Finn (finnder.itch.io) is described in search snippets as a "bare bones and expandable FPS Controller". The same snippets give a release of September 26, 2025, GPLv3 code and an HTML5 platform. When fetched on 2026-11-03, the page was password-protected ("A password is required to view this page"), so it is not currently purchasable. — [finnder.itch.io/godot-fps-engine](https://finnder.itch.io/godot-fps-engine)

### Inferences
- The name similarity to Cowsins' product is likely coincidental. It is a basic controller, not a full kit.

### Gaps
- I did not check whether Cowsins has announced Godot plans, for example on their Discord or website.
