How to play

It's normal chess, with one change: you can't just take a piece. You have to beat it in a first-person duel.

  • Win the duel as the attacker and you take the square, like a normal capture.
  • Lose and your attacking piece is gone, and the defender stays where it is.
  • If the clock runs out (75 s), the defender holds its square.
  • Checkmate wins as usual. If your king attacks and loses, or a failed attack leaves your king in check, you lose.

Controls: drag on the left to move, drag on the right to look, hold 🔥 to shoot. A hit on the top of a piece (its crown) does 1.5× damage.
