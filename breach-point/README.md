# Breach Point

A mobile first-person shooter made with Godot 4.7: a Kriss Vector held by
animated gloved hands (draw, shoot and reload clips from the model), plus
procedural recoil, sway, aim down sights and inspect, enemy soldiers and touch
controls.

## Download

Every push builds an APK: grab the newest `breach-point-v0.1.N` release from the
repo's Releases page and tap `breach-point.apk`.

## Controls (phone)

- Left side: move (push the stick up past the ring to sprint)
- Right side: drag to look; the FIRE button also turns the view while held
- AIM toggles aim down sights; CROUCH while sprinting slides
- RELOAD, JUMP, INSPECT; II pauses (sensitivity slider)

Keyboard/mouse also works on a computer: WASD, Shift, Space, C, R, F, mouse.

## Layout

- `scripts/` - all game code (built in code, one small scene)
  - `weapon.gd` gun, firing, recoil, ADS, reload and inspect
  - `player.gd` movement, `bot.gd` enemies, `map.gd` arena, `hud.gd`, `touch.gd`
- `assets/models/kriss/` - the animated weapon and hands
- `assets/sounds/` - generated by `tools/gen_sounds.py`

Test without a phone: `godot --path . -- --autotest=<folder>` plays through
firing, aiming, reloading, inspecting and a bot fight and saves screenshots.
